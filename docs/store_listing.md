## Short Description (80 chars max)
**Smart assistant for the visually impaired. Object detection, OCR, and Navigation.**

## Full Description (4000 chars max)
AIVA (AI Vision Assistant) empowers visually impaired users by describing the world around them using advanced AI.

### Identify Your Surroundings
- **Object Detection**: Instantly recognize people, obstacles, and objects in your path.
- **Scene Description**: Get detailed AI-generated descriptions of complex scenes.
- **OCR (Text Reading)**: Read signs, documents, and labels aloud.

### Navigate safely
- **Avoid Obstacles**: Receive real-time audio warnings about nearby hazards.
- **Depth Perception**: Understand the distance to objects in your environment.

### Stay Connected
- **Emergency SOS**: Trigger an immediate distress signal to your contacts with your precise location.
- **Voice Commands**: Control the app entirely hands-free using natural language commands like "Where am I?", "Read this", or "Help me".

### Privacy First
AIVA processes sensitive data on your own private server. Your images and audio are never shared with third parties or advertisers.

### Permissions
- **Camera**: Essential for vision features.
- **Microphone**: Required for voice commands.
- **Location**: Used for "Where am I" queries and emergency SOS.
- **SMS**: Used to send SOS alerts to your contacts.
